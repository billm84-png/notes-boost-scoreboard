Your playbook

Six ways to get more from every boost

These come straight from what has worked in this room so far. Small habits, real results.

  1. Drop your Note earlyNotes dropped before 11am got about … replies from members in their first day. Later ones got about ….
  2. Keep it text onlyNotes without an image drew about … member replies, against about … for Notes with one. Words start conversations.
  3. Say it in 75 words or fewerShort Notes give people something to respond to without feeling like an article. Give enough context, then stop.
  4. Comment on at least 3The boost works because everyone gives. Start with the Notes that have the fewest replies so far.
  5. Answer your repliesWhen someone comments on your Note, reply back. That's where the relationship starts.
  6. Come back in the afternoonLate arrivals are easy to miss. A second pass up the thread keeps everyone in the conversation.

How we count

We read the boost threads and the Notes linked in them after each boost. "Showed up" means you dropped a link to your own Note. Replies from members count people who also dropped a Note that day and replied within 24 hours. Most Generous counts how many other members' Notes you replied to after they were dropped. Welcome Crew counts replies to Notes dropped after 11am Eastern or by someone at their first boost. Subscriber and follower totals come from each member's public Substack profile, counted once per person. Coaches (Derek, Deren, Stephanie, Stephanie and Bill) help run the room, so they aren't ranked. Early days: with only a few boosts so far, read these as encouragement, not a final verdict.
